# Open loops

Every ask in flight (to `infra`, to a topic, to the user). The PM keeps this current and chases from it (`PM.md`, Accountability and follow-up). An entry leaves when the evidence of the answer is seen, not when someone says it is done. Each entry has a who, a what, a date asked and a next chase date; nothing is "waiting" without them.

## Owed to the PM (asks to sessions)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Owed to the user (decisions and actions only they can take)

Lead with the most important. Each as a decision: the question, the options, the PM's recommendation.

| Decision or action | Options and recommendation | Where | Asked | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Reported to the user first, ahead of what is going well.
