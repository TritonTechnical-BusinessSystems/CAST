# Who reports to whom

The rule: **every session knows who it reports to**. The PM, `infra` and every topic session report to the PM, and through it to the user. A session without a seat and without a topic (titled with ⚪) also reports to the PM. It sends the PM what it learns and asks before touching an area a topic has claimed.

The PM keeps this table current: new sessions are added when they check in, finished ones are marked, and a topic that is merged and retired is marked done. Names are as other sessions address them; the worktree id identifies a session whose name changed.

`ROSTER.md` is the short who-is-who every session loads (role, address, machine, job). This file is the history behind it: reachability, replaced sessions, sessions without a seat.

## Seats and topics

All report to the PM. See `ROLES.md`, `ROSTER.md` and the seat and topic issues for who holds what.
